# Owner decision: structure, audience gate, discovery (2026-10-26)

Binding owner statement; supersedes the evergreen part of 2026-10-26-bible-evergreen-proposal.md. Not to be re-discussed; changes need a new owner decision.

## 1. Top-level structure of GCOS and the Hub
**Time Sensitive:** Haber, Duyuru, Burs, Eğitim, Research (current / new).
**Evergreen:** Sağlık & Referans, Research Rediscovery, Dossier, Doktorlar, Protokoller, İstatistik.
**Keşif (Discovery):** a continuous layer that proposes NEW candidates for every area above: new statistics sources (matching the statistics definition), new protocols (protocol definition), new dossiers (dossier definition), new doctor candidates (doctor definition), new research-rediscovery areas, and new Haber/Duyuru/Burs/Eğitim/Research sources. It scans fields useful for Turkish medical professionals, students, and people interested in healthy living, longevity and chronic disease, and brings candidates for approval.

## 2. Hard gate #1: audience fit
Audience fit is the first, non-negotiable gate (to be written at position 1 of the Bible, ahead of safety/accuracy in the conflict order of §1.3 and as the first hard gate in §1.2). Nothing that does not fit our audiences is wanted, in any area.

Audiences (owner definitions; may be redefined later by the owner):
- **Kaduse Medikal:** medical students; built on stethoscope sales, developing other medical areas from it.
- **Türk Tıp Topluluğu:** medical professionals; to be grown later and indirectly cooperate with Kaduse Medikal.
- **Sağlık kanalı (İyileşme Kanalı):** everyone living in Türkiye who cares about health.

Example: a scholarship in Canada that does not address medical professionals and students in Türkiye is not wanted. (Consistent with Bible §12 `application_eligibility` = YES for Türkiye.)

## 3. Lane purity
No news in Duyuru, no announcements in Haber, no new research under another heading. Each heading builds its own operational excellence against its own framework and audience, and improves through feedback (ratings, learner). No cross-heading leakage; type mismatch is moved to the right heading or rejected.

## 4. Consequences (tracked in 2026-10-24-hub-full-audit.md)
- Bible v4.1: add audience fit as hard gate #1; add time-sensitivity axis with the two groups; `referans`/evergreen families; Keşif layer definitions; audience definitions (§22.1 and channel layer).
- Evergreen group items (Dossier, Doktorlar, Protokoller, İstatistik) are no longer Haber-like in any count or gate.
- Keşif proposals must pass the audience gate before they reach the owner.
