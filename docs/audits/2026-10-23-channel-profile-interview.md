# Channel profile interview (2026-10-23, working record)

Purpose: define, from the owner's own answers, how Global OS decides what is relevant for each of its three channels. Nothing here is implemented yet; the answers become the channel profiles that the filtering and routing rules are written from. Status: **in progress** (questions answered so far are marked).

## Context given by the owner
Three channels, three Instagram accounts:
1. **Kaduse Medikal**: stethoscope brand, but a feed of attention-grabbing health developments to connect with an audience (Kaduse News, Kaduse Research).
2. **Tıp Topluluğu**: Turkish health professionals; announcements, jobs, news, research; to gain followers; loosely linked to Kaduse behind the scenes, later usable for Kaduse sales.
3. **İyileşme Kanalı**: Turkish general public interested in health; popular, high wow-factor research and news about their own health; Dossier, Doctors and Protocols modules mostly serve this audience; later ads and partnerships.

Current gap: the item selection does not serve these three well (US-centred regulatory and trade news, country-specific studies, news mixed into Research, basic science).

## Answers
**Q1. Can one item go to several channels?** Yes by content type:
- Research and News: may go to **all three**, by content.
- Announcement (Duyuru), Scholarship (Burs), Education (Eğitim): **Tıp Topluluğu only**.
- Evergreen: usually **İyileşme Kanalı** and **Tıp Topluluğu**, by content.
=> an item carries a *set* of channels, with fixed channels for Duyuru/Burs/Eğitim.

**Q2. What is relevant? (sample headlines)**
- Relevant for **all three channels**: a new blood test predicting Alzheimer's years ahead; AI spotting cancer on lung films before a physician; a well-run trial with a negative result (vitamin D and flu); Turkish researchers' diabetes drug trial; sitting time offset by a weekly walk.
- **Never relevant**: a single country's regulatory approval (an FDA drug approval); a study specific to one country or region (adolescent diets in a Kenyan informal settlement).
- Stated reason: Turkish readers and Turkish health professionals do not care about US/Canada/Kenya-local matters.
=> rule: **universally applicable biological or clinical findings qualify; country-specific policy, regulation, trade or local studies do not.** Negative-result rigorous studies qualify.

**Q3. İyileşme Kanalı audience.** General public that puts its own health first; people seeking solutions for **chronic diseases**; people interested in **longevity**; people who want to follow health developments. Topic emphasis: **nutrition, sleep, cardiovascular, diabetes, mental health**; **longevity in all areas**; no single priority otherwise: "all attention-grabbing areas". **Exclude: cancer-treatment promises.**
