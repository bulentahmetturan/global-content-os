## Shared Doctor / Protocol discovery kernel (local, unreleased)
- Additive schema and Worker APIs are in migration `0044_shared_discovery_kernel.sql`; it has not been applied remotely. Owner decisions use `HUB_OPERATOR_TOKEN`; the local onboarding executor uses the separate `DISCOVERY_EXECUTOR_TOKEN`.
- The Hub queue exposes candidate names and APPROVE / WATCH / REJECT (Protocol candidates also support the protocol-specific merge/reject outcomes). Admission compilation is required before a candidate reaches the queue; unsupported sources remain manual/restricted and zero-usable-source candidates stay NOT_READY.
- `scripts/discovery/compile-admission.mjs --candidate <id> --input <json>` runs read-only source-lifecycle plans and writes per-source D4 artifacts to `.logs/discovery-admission/`. Its gate extraction maps source-lifecycle's actual `IDENTITY`, `ACCESS`, `ENDPOINT`, `PARSER`, and `CANARY` results. Stale evidence/package mismatch triggers a local source-lifecycle re-plan in the executor.
- An owner approval creates an immutable intent only. Execute it locally with `node scripts/discovery/onboarding-executor.mjs --once`; source changes remain subject to the existing lifecycle and D4 controls. Do not configure periodic polling or a scheduled wave.
- The local executor resolves/creates canonical Doctors through the actor registry and Protocols through the protocol registry (including alias and family-variant outcomes), then links eligible sources through existing domain associations. Manual, restricted, or failed individual sources are isolated as partial coverage; they do not block entity creation or progress from an active source. Retry-safe progress is recorded only after a routed source item exists, then advances through `PIPELINE_ACTIVE` and `PRODUCTION_ACTIVE` when the item reaches the Global Hub. Protocol items link to definition claims as `CONTEXT`, not efficacy evidence.
- Local D1 end-to-end tests cover Doctor and Protocol approval through canonical entity, ingest, and Hub item; alias/variant merging; and partial manual-source coverage. Cross-feed signal persistence is best-effort and cannot change an otherwise successful ingest/evidence result. The kernel remains local and unreleased; migration `0044` is unapplied remotely, waves remain manual one-shots, and no periodic scheduler is configured.
- Doctor ingestion can submit `unknownProtocolMentions`; Protocol evidence capture can submit `unknownActorMentions`. They emit deduped cross-feed signals only; neither domain creates the other domain's entities.
- Wave metrics and readiness pass rate are recorded per manual run. Doctors switch to maintenance after three low-yield waves; Protocol discovery saturates after three zero-new-canonical waves, then maintenance is quarterly or event-triggered.
